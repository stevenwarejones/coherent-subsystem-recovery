# A recovery obstruction without endpoint perfection

Independent AI derivation with separate exact and numerical checks (see [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md)). Human expert review and
historical novelty remain outstanding.


## Evidence status

The rational certificate and mutations are checked in this repository. Physical
realization, controller constraints, and order coverage remain prose proofs for
independent review. The older endpoint-conditioned certificates are retained in
`history/endpoint_forward_queries/`. No all-finite-query or novelty claim is made.

## Result and scope

The earlier five-slot certificate bounded only controllers that recovered the ideal Bell/Pauli instance perfectly. The new certificate imposes no endpoint condition and no endpoint-kernel reduction. It bounds every causal controller using the chronological slot order V,U,V,U,V.

There is a finite-dimensional physical protocol instance with measured score f=9/10 for which every such controller has

\[
r\le\frac{1692197}{2000000}=0.8460985
<0.85=\frac{3f-1}{2}.
\]

The gap is exactly 7803/2000000=0.0039015. This is a conservative certified bound, not a claimed exact optimum of the restricted recovery problem. The numerical optimization gave approximately 0.84608845347 before rational certification.

The controller may have arbitrary finite workspace and arbitrary controller operations between calls. It may be designed specifically for this score. The result excludes a useful class of score-selected algorithms that the previous endpoint argument did not cover. There is no restriction on its performance at f=1.

The theorem still restricts the interface: only forward controlled interventions touch M; the initial controller is independent of the encoded state; no bypass/reference access, extra target gates, or postselection. Query order is fixed. The supplied arithmetic and numerical checks pass, but the physical construction and causal-history argument require independent mathematical review.

## 1. Exact input data

Let W be the 20 distinct positive words generated by subsequences of the five slots. The certificate contains a 40-by-40 rational positive-definite moment matrix

\[
\Gamma_{(a,w),(b,v)}=\langle w\psi_a,v\psi_b\rangle.
\]

The two empty-word diagonal entries sum to one. The entries obey exact unitary consistency: Gamma_(a,xw),(b,xv)=Gamma_(a,w),(b,v) whenever all words involved are in W.

The finite realization argument is short. Factor Gamma into vectors spanning a space S of dimension 40. For each generator x, send v_(a,w) to v_(a,xw). Inner-product consistency makes this map a well-defined isometry on its domain span, including all linear dependencies. Extend it to a unitary on S. Suffix closure of W proves by induction that the resulting U,V and psi=sum_a |a>v_(a,empty) realize every moment. The reference marginal of psi need not initially be maximally mixed.

Define

\[
L=I+X\otimes U+Z\otimes V,\qquad K=L^\dagger L/9,
\quad p=\langle\psi|K|\psi\rangle.
\]

The exact rational p is stored and recomputed by the verifier; numerically p is approximately 0.6684440008871143. Put

\[
\theta=\frac{1/10}{1-p}.
\]

Then 0<theta<1 and (1-theta)+theta p=9/10.

## 2. Physical state and common-decoder score

First mix the adversarial state with an ideal Bell/Pauli block: weight theta on psi and weight 1-theta on Phi, with interventions U direct-sum X and V direct-sum Z. These blocks live in a target space of dimension 42.

Now add a four-valued classical flag inside M. For each Pauli g, conjugate the reference by g and multiply the two intervention blocks by the signs s_X(g), s_Z(g) defined by gXg-adjoint=s_X(g)X and similarly for Z. Average the four flagged blocks. The reference marginal becomes I/2.

For the coherent test, C=Tr_M(L rho L-adjoint)/9 becomes (f/2)I after this flagging. The optimized common-decoder formula

\[
f_{\rm opt}=\frac{(\operatorname{Tr}\sqrt C)^2}{2}
\]

therefore gives the actually attainable score f=9/10. It is not an assumption that the measured f equals the support score p for an arbitrary state. A purification of this flagged state gives a valid encoding from a Bell input, with the purifying system placed in the unrestricted bypass.

This direct construction uses dimension 168. It can be reduced to 162: the four ideal blocks are operationally equivalent to one ideal two-dimensional block for every allowed controller, even without endpoint perfection. Their Pauli sign changes are conjugations of the target Pauli operators, and the corresponding reference rotation is the transposed target rotation on Phi. Every word transforms covariantly; the final target rotation disappears upon discarding M. Consequently the ideal block's recovery fidelity is unchanged by this Pauli symmetrization. The numerical bridge check confirms this identity, but the covariance argument is the proof.

The unitaries can be held fixed while the state weight theta varies. Access to a flag measurement on M is not supplied: M remains accessible only through the interventions.

## 3. Controller optimization with no endpoint kernel

There are 32 binary histories. Purify all controller operations and let c_(a,e,h) be the amplitude of output qubit a, discarded controller environment e, and history h. Set

\[
T_{ah,bk}=\sum_e c_{a,e,h}\overline{c_{b,e,k}}\succeq0.
\]

T has size 64. Its output trace G has zero cross-block between histories with different last query bits. Sum the equal-bit diagonal blocks and repeat until the remaining scalar is one. These linear equalities are necessary for every allowed controller; no upper bound on its workspace is imposed. The certificate does not need the converse circuit-realization theorem to establish its upper bound.

The moment matrix determines a rational coefficient matrix C_adv satisfying r_adv=Tr(C_adv T). The ideal Bell/Pauli instance gives another rational matrix C_0, directly from its Born amplitudes. Thus the unflagged mixture objective is

\[
\operatorname{Tr}(C_\theta T),\qquad
C_\theta=\theta C_{\rm adv}+(1-\theta)C_0.
\]

For a physical controller D on the flagged instance, its score equals this objective evaluated on its Pauli-symmetrized history matrix. Symmetrization preserves positivity and every causal constraint and adds no query slots. Since the dual bound holds for all T, it holds for that symmetrized T in particular. Ideal-block covariance permits the 162-dimensional compressed construction too.

## 4. Rational dual certificate

Write the causal constraints as Tr(A_i T)=b_i. All b_i are zero except the final normalization, which equals one. The certificate supplies rational dual multipliers and alpha=1692197/2000000 such that

\[
R=\alpha I+\sum_{i:\,b_i=0}\lambda_i A_i-C_\theta\succ0.
\]

Therefore every causal positive T satisfies

\[
r=\operatorname{Tr}(C_\theta T)
\le\alpha-\operatorname{Tr}(RT)\le\alpha.
\]

There is no B Y B-transpose endpoint parametrization here. The matrix is verified on the full 64-dimensional controller-history space. Complex controller coefficients are covered: C_theta and the constraints are real, so the real part of a feasible Hermitian T remains positive semidefinite and has the same real objective.

`proofs/verify_robust_forward_obstruction.py` is standard-library exact rational arithmetic. It reconstructs C_adv, C_0, theta, p, and the causal dual lift. It checks positive definiteness of Gamma and R, every moment identity, and the reported gap. Five mutations reject for specified reasons: nonpositive moments, inconsistent unitary moments, a false mixture weight, an invalid dual bound, and a false reported deficit. Python -O is refused.

## 5. A whole high-score interval, not only f=0.9

For any f in [9/10,1], choose theta_f=(1-f)/(1-p). Let lambda=(1-f)/(1-9/10), so theta_f=lambda theta. For each fixed controller, the physical mixture score is affine in theta_f. Its ideal-block recovery is at most one, without assuming equality. Hence

\[
r_f\le(1-\lambda)+\lambda\alpha
=1-\frac{307803}{200000}(1-f).
\]

The coefficient is 1.539015. The existential sharp target is 1-1.5(1-f), so the certified gap is at least

\[
\frac{7803}{200000}(1-f)=0.039015(1-f).
\]

For every f<1 in this interval the gap is positive. At f=1 it vanishes. The bound holds for every controller separately at each f, so choosing a different controller for different scores cannot evade it within this query pattern. This is not a fixed positive error floor as f approaches one, nor a dimension-asymptotic lower bound.

## 6. Three active calls, with preselected random order

Every mixed binary word of length at most three is a subsequence of V,U,V,U,V. Any fixed three-call pattern using both generators, with arbitrary intermediate controller processing, therefore embeds by skipping the unused slots.

A circuit using only one generator is entanglement breaking from M to the output controller: diagonalize that generator, observe that all history words preserve its eigenspaces, and trace out M. Only the classical eigenvalue label can influence the controller. Its Bell recovery fidelity is at most 1/2 on any input state. This is below the certified upper bound throughout f in [0.9,1].

A preselected random mixture of such circuits cannot improve their common upper bound. Therefore the robust three-query exclusion covers fixed orders, preselected random orders, and selection of their coefficients/order mixture based on the known score. It does not claim coverage of arbitrary outcome-dependent query order, coherent control of causal order, extra target access, or postselected recovery.

## 7. Numerical bridge checks

`tests/verify_robust_forward_bridge.py` constructs the finite unitaries from the rational moment matrix, then generates 30 actual random controller circuits with controller dimensions 2,4,8. For each it independently computes the Bell success amplitudes on every flagged branch and compares them with Tr(C_theta T_sym). The maximum discrepancy was approximately 2.8e-16. The scalar-C identity agreed to approximately 1.7e-16.

A deliberate omission of the reference Pauli rotation changes the result by approximately 0.0201 in the test set. Conversely, collapsing the ideal flags changes nothing to numerical precision, as required by covariance. An initial test incorrectly expected the latter operation to fail; the equality revealed the covariance simplification, and the check now tests the correct claim. These floating-point checks corroborate tensor ordering and normalization; they are not exact proofs of universal physical realizability.

## 8. What remains open

No all-finite-query obstruction has been proved. Four calls have not been shown sufficient. The numerical searches for the best universal affine slope at larger query counts encountered inaccurate statuses and a solver failure; no new theorem is inferred from those outputs.

The next general target is a count-indexed family of causal duals or an inversion/state-conversion reduction whose oracle access is valid. A small positive gap for one query count does not establish a recurrence. The independent review should concentrate on the moment realization, the physical common-decoder construction, the full-history dual, and the exact algorithm class covered by padding.
